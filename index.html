Board-to-X Sync

A personal automation that helps me share Pins from my own Pinterest board to my own X (Twitter) account.

What it does

  1. Once a day, reads the public RSS feed of a single Pinterest board owned by the operator.
  2. Emails the operator a link for each new Pin that opens X's post screen with the Pin's title and a link back to the original Pin.
  3. The operator reviews and posts each one manually. On mobile, the share page can attach the Pin image via the device's share menu.

Who it's for

Board-to-X Sync is built and operated by an individual developer for their own personal use. It is not a public service and is not offered to other users.

What it does not do

  • It does not use the Pinterest API or the X API, and does not log in to either service.
  • It never posts automatically, and never creates, edits, or deletes Pins or boards.
  • It never accesses other users' data or any non-public data.
